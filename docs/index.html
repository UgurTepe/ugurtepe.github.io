Uğur Tepe's Personal Website

I'm Uğur Tepe, a master's student in physics at Heinrich-Heine University, Düsseldorf, Germany.

My master's thesis focuses on topological quantum computing, specifically on so-called Majorana box qubits (MBQ). Previously, I studied ways to reduce the sample complexity in quantum algorithms.

Research Interests

  • Theoretical Condensed Matter
  • Topological Quantum Computing
  • Quantum Information
