About Uğur Tepe

I'm Uğur Tepe, a master's student in physics at Heinrich-Heine University Düsseldorf. My interests lie in theoretical condensed matter physics and topological quantum computing.

You can download my curriculum vitae. PDF · 110 kB

Research Background

In my bachelor's thesis, I investigated adaptive methods for estimating quantum expectation values using the empirical Bernstein stopping rule. I adapted the algorithm to the quantum computing setting, developed a Python implementation, and tested the approach through quantum chemistry simulations. This work was later developed into a paper in the Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation (2025).

Research Group & Current Work

I'm currently working on my master's thesis in Reinhold Egger's group at the Institute for Theoretical Physics IV, Heinrich-Heine University Düsseldorf (HHU). My research focuses on Majorana box qubits and topological quantum computing within theoretical condensed matter physics.

My earlier work on quantum measurement sampling is described in my bachelor's thesis on quantum expectation value estimation and the accompanying empirical Bernstein algorithm in Python. See my publications and preprints for the resulting paper.
