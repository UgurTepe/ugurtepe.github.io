About Uğur Tepe

I'm a master's student in physics in Reinhold Egger's group at HHU Düsseldorf, working on Majorana box qubits.

Research Background

In my bachelor's thesis, I investigated adaptive methods for estimating quantum expectation values using the empirical Bernstein stopping rule. I adapted the algorithm to the quantum computing setting, developed a Python implementation, and tested the approach through quantum chemistry simulations. This work was later developed into a paper in the Journal of Chemical Theory and Computation (2025).
